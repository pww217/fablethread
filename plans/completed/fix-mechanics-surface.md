# Plan: Fix beats, pressures, and narration directives in prompts

## Problem

Three mechanics are not surfacing correctly in the extract prompts:

1. **GM_BEAT** — beats 2-5 are empty in progress user prompt. The beats are generated by rules engine (visible in rules output as "Narration Directive: Breathe"), but not surfaced in the progress prompt. Beats at 6-7 work because they were carried forward as pending beats.

2. **SCENE_PRESSURE** — header disappears at turns 3-5 and 7. Should persist when pressures are active. Turn 2 shows `[mortar_threat_attracted]`, turn 6 shows `[mortar_arrival_imminent]`, but turns 3-5 and 7 have no header at all.

3. **NARRATION_DIRECTIVE** — computed in `narrate_user.j2` based on deescalate, scene_pressure urgency counts, and age thresholds, but not surfaced in any extract prompt.

## Root causes

### 1. GM_BEAT empty on beats 2-5

The beats at 2-5 are generated by the rules engine and stored in `state.meta.pending_gm_beat` after narration. However, the beats are **cleared after narration** (turn.py:611) and **restored before extraction** (turn.py:616). The extraction pipeline should see them via `pending_beat` in the progress user prompt.

The issue is that beats 2-5 were **consumed** by the rules engine (they were "Narration Directive: Breathe" — not beats, just directives). The rules engine at beats 2-5 did NOT emit a `gm_beat` in its JSON output — it only emitted a `directive` string. The beats at 6-7 were emitted by the **progress extractor** at turn 5 (visible in state deltas: `progress.gm_beat` and `progress.beat_disposition = replace`).

**The beats at 2-5 are genuinely empty** — the rules engine didn't generate beats, it generated directives. The beats should come from the progress extractor, not the rules engine. The issue is that beats 2-5's progress extractors didn't generate beats.

**Fix:** The progress extractor needs beats guidance. Check if beats 2-5 had beats guidance in the progress user prompt. The beats guidance comes from:
- `pending_beat` (carried from previous turn) — not applicable for beats 2-5
- `deescalate` — beats 2-5 had no deescalate
- `scene_pressure` — beats 2-5 had no scene_pressure header
- `rules_stakes` — beats 2-5 had stakes

The beats guidance at beats 2-5 was essentially: stakes + no pending beat + no deescalate + no pressures. The progress extractor had no reason to generate beats.

**This may be working as intended** — beats are only generated when there's a reason (pending beat, deescalate, or pressures). The beats at 6-7 were generated because turn 5's rules outcome had a PARTIAL band with narrative consequence, and the progress extractor at turn 5 generated beats.

**Verdict:** GM_BEAT may be working as intended. The beats at 2-5 were empty because there was no pending beat, no deescalate, and no pressures to drive beat generation. The beats at 6-7 were generated by the progress extractor at turn 5 and carried forward.

### 2. SCENE_PRESSURE header missing at turns 3-5 and 7

The `scene_pressure` header in the progress user prompt is rendered from `extraction_ctx.scene_pressure_this_turn` (extraction.py:475). This is computed by `_build_extraction_context()` which applies scene/state deltas in-memory.

The header is missing at turns 3-5 and 7 because `scene_pressure` is empty at those turns. Turn 2 had `[mortar_threat_attracted]` which should have persisted. Turn 6 had `[mortar_arrival_imminent]` which should have persisted to 7.

**Root cause:** The pressure `[mortar_threat_attracted]` from turn 2 was removed at turn 3 (visible in events.jsonl or state deltas). The pressure `[mortar_arrival_imminent]` at turn 6 was removed at turn 7 (visible in state deltas: `progress.scene_pressure_remove = mortar_arrival_imminent` at turn 7).

**Fix:** Check why pressures are being removed. The `_expire_scene_pressures()` function should escalate background→building at 6 turns, building→immediate at 10 turns, not remove them. The default 8-turn TTL should keep them alive. Check if pressures are being removed by the compaction process or by the progress extractor.

### 3. NARRATION_DIRECTIVE not surfaced in extract prompts

The narration directives (Breathe, Overwhelm, Pressure, Tension) are computed in `narrate_user.j2` based on deescalate, scene_pressure urgency counts, and age thresholds. They are NOT passed to the extract prompts.

**Fix:** Add narration directive context to the progress user prompt. The directive can inform beats/pressure decisions.
