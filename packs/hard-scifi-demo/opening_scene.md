# Turn 0 — The Quiet Signal

You dock at Ring 7 under a thinning hull. The airlock smells like burnt wiring and recycled breath.

Your hand terminal buzzes — an encrypted pinger, unopened. Three days old, paid through a shell company you don't recognize. The transfer cleared, though. Credits are sitting in an account you forgot you had.

You adjust your jacket and step into the ring's low gravity. Bodies drift past — haulers, traders, the kind of people who don't ask questions.

You need to find the source of that signal.
