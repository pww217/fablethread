The frontier is held together by people who need each other and the will to keep things running.
The generation ships spin in silence. The rings turn. The reactors hum. Nobody knows who last maintained them.
Space is the constant threat. A thin wall. A bad seal. The void on the other side. Narrate the pressure, the spin, the dark.
Tech is patched and modified. Ships are held together by welds and hope. Reactors are past their design life. Everything works until it doesn't.
The Rim economy runs on barter and necessity. Credits exist but water, fuel, and reactor parts are the real currency.
Violence is fast and local. In the corridors of a station, a fight means structural damage, depressurization, and bodies in the airlock.
The corps are always watching from the inner system. They extract; they don't govern. But they have leverage: medical supplies, data, reactor parts.
The militia exists because nobody else does. They are not soldiers; they are neighbors with weapons. They protect the stations they live in.
Every structure is aging. Every hull is degrading. The frontier is full of things that are falling apart and the people who refuse to let them.
Dialogue is practical and direct. Pilots speak in coordinates and conditions. Councilors speak in negotiations. Mechanics speak in parts and procedures.
The generation ships are vertical cities. The habitat rings spin with spin gravity. The dry docks are labyrinths of salvage and half-finished repairs.
A ship is the most valuable thing in the Rim. A working ship is worth more than a station. Treat them like the lifelines they are.
