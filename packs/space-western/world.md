# World Bible — The Rim (Year 2187)

The inner system is corporate territory — Mars a factory, Ceres a data-hub, Luna a military post. The Rim is what comes after: the outer Belt, Jovian moons, Kuiper fringe. The corps' reach ends here. The Rim is autonomous by necessity, not ideology. The corps just ran out of fuel for their patrols.

The Rim is built on remnants: generation ship habitats drifting through the Jovian system, asteroid stations inside hollowed-out rock, orbital rings around gas giants that nobody maintains. The technology works until it doesn't. The ships are patched together from salvage. The people are the same.
