# World Bible — Neo-Synth (Year 2087)

Neo-Synth is a megalopolis of twelve million, built on the ruins of old coastal cities, divided into sectors: the Corporate Spire with clean air and private security, the Mid-Ring with insurance-grade surveillance, the Undercity in flooded lower streets, and the Sprawl beyond the flood line. The rain is constant and acidic.

National governments exist but exert control through corporate charters. The real power belongs to megacorporations — each controls an entire sector, employs entire neighborhoods, and answers to no elected body. The cold war between Arasaka-Müller (defense, cyberware) and Militech-NOV (logistics, drones) runs the city's blood.
