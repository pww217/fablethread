The city never sleeps and it never forgets. Every neon sign, every data-stream, every face is watched.
Corp architecture is cold and precise. Street architecture is improvised and desperate.
Cyberware has consequences — rejection, dependency, the slow erosion of what makes you human.
Netrunning is dangerous. ICE kills. Counter-ICE is rarer and deadlier.
Credits are universal but data is worth more. A secret can buy a sector; a leak can buy a life.
Violence is fast and documented. Corporate security has cameras and drones; street violence has witnesses and recordings.
The rain is constant. The neon reflects. The air tastes of ozone and synthetic protein.
Corp NPCs speak in measured precision. Street NPCs speak in shortcuts and slang. Augmented NPCs have physical tells — the click of an optic, the hum of a neural booster.
No one is who they say they are. Not the fixer, not the corp, not the contact. Trust is earned in small increments.
The body is just hardware. What matters is what you do with it — and who's paying for the power.
A deal gone wrong is more dangerous than a corp hit. The people you work with are always calculating their own exit.
