# World Bible — Allied 1940–1945

The war is a machine of logistics — ammunition, fuel, spare parts, food, medical supplies — and the side that moves them best wins. Every engagement is decided before the first shot by supply officers, quartermasters, transport drivers, and the civilians who keep the factories running.

The Allies are united by necessity, not ideology: American firepower and mass production, British intelligence and improvisation, Canadian and ANZAC experience, Free European forces fighting for homelands that still exist on paper — all operating under different chains of command, different doctrines, and strained cooperation.
