# World Bible — Golden Age of Piracy 1715–1725

The Caribbean is a theater of war between empires, and pirates exploit the gaps. Britain, Spain, France, and Holland fight over colonies, trade routes, and sugar profits. Pirates prey on the merchant ships that carry the wealth — and sometimes fight alongside one empire against another when it suits them.

Pirate communities like Nassau and Port Royal operated as self-governing havens with their own rules, courts, and distribution systems. The Articles — a written code agreed to by the crew — covered voting rights, compensation for injuries, and shares of plunder. It was the most democratic society many of these people ever knew.
