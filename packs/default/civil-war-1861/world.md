# World Bible — The Divided States (1861)

April 1861, Fort Sumter has fallen. The Union holds the North with the Navy, railroads, factories, and twice the population. The Confederacy controls the South with the rivers, cavalry, and defensive terrain. The border states — Maryland, Kentucky, Missouri — are split by loyalty and blood.

This is not a war of clean lines but of rail junctions, river crossings, supply depots, and telegraph lines. The real war is logistical — who can feed their army, replace their ammunition, sustain their volunteers. Rifled muskets, ironclads, and the telegraph define the new reality.
