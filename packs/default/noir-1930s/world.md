# World Bible — Noir 1930s

The Great Depression is the ground, not a backdrop: 20–25% unemployment, New Deal programs new and uncertain, cash and favors as currency. Prohibition ended in 1933 but the bootlegging infrastructure remains — organized crime diversified into labor racketeering, gambling, loan-sharking, protection.

The city is a collection of overlapping jurisdictions — city police, county sheriff, state troopers, federal agents, private detectives — each with different scope, different rules, different people on the payroll. The police force is a mix of idealists and careerists, with a significant portion taking payback.
