# World Bible — The Drowned Earth

Sea level rose two hundred feet over forty years — glacial collapse, permafrost methane, the cause is contested. Sixty years since the first evacuations, the year is 2147. The flood is not receding. The map has been redrawn by water.

The world is defined by elevation: the Highlands hold the communities on mountain towns and plateau cities, the Edge is the contested transition zone where flood meets land, and the Flooded Zones are the drowned ruins — skyscrapers as reefs, highways as submerged lanes, suburbs as kelp forests — where divers salvage and floaters trade.
