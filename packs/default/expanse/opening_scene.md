# Welcome aboard the *Wretch*

**Year 2351. Sol system.** Earth and Mars stand toe-to-toe across the inner system, signing treaties through clenched teeth and drawing red lines through the orbits between them. Out past the asteroid belt, the OPA — the Outer Planets Alliance, what's left of it — bleeds itself in factional knife fights from Hygiea to Europa. You don't follow politics. Politics catches up either way.

You captain the **Wretch** — a forty-year-old Mowteng-class light freighter, Belter-built and Belter-broken. Hull patched in three places. Port maneuvering thruster sticking by 200 milliseconds. Water recycler wheezing like a dying old dock-rat on the night shift. Slow. Crappy. Yours. She runs because you and your crew make her run.

Right now she's sitting in cradle **41-B at Ceres Station**, taking on dock fees you can't afford. **Ceres**: ten kilometers of rock spun for one-third gravity, three million Belters packed into corridors that smell like fryer oil and ozone and stim-tea. *Star Helix Security* has the badges; the *OPA* has the locker rooms; on the **Lower Levels Market** — where you are right now — money has the only real authority.

Your crew is two:

- **Davi Moreau-Ng** — pilot. Half-Earther, half-Martian, all attitude. Walked off an MCRN destroyer two years back with classified flight maps and a perfect understanding of inner-system patrol corridors. Currently asleep in the *Wretch's* galley, head-down on the table. Won't be roused for less than a fire.
- **Sigge Petrescu** — first mate and enforcer. Tall even for a Belter, scarred jaw, soft voice. Used to bounce drunks at a Pallas Station dive bar. Now bounces bigger problems for you. She's at the cargo ramp, watching the corridor, two guns on her hip and a kowmang knife on her belt.

Last run was a milk-run: six crates of black-market water filtration cores, Tycho to Ceres. Paid for itself, barely. The credits in your account are short of clearing the dock *and* refueling. Pick one. The harbormaster's clock is ticking — **twenty hours**, then the *Wretch* gets seized and auctioned for parts.

So you came down here to the market. Taverns. Merchants. Job-board terminals. Whispered handshakes in the back corridors. A Belter captain looking for work. *Beltalowda looks out for beltalowda* — when it's convenient.

The crowd thrums around you. A Ceresian busker with a salvaged guitar plays a piece of broken music. The smell of fried tubers and cheap whiskey drifts out of an open tavern doorway. Somewhere, a child laughs. Somewhere closer, two men with OPA tattoos watch you over their drinks.

The clock starts now. *Where do you go, Captain?*
