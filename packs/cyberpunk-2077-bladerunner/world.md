# World Bible — Neo-Synth (Year 2087)

## The Setting

Neo-Synth is a megalopolis of twelve million souls, built on the ruins of old coastal cities. The skyline is a wall of black glass and neon — corporate towers rising above flooded street level. The rain is constant, acidic, and reflects every holographic advertisement. The city is divided into sectors: the Corporate Spire (upper levels, clean air, private security), the Mid-Ring (commercial, residential, insurance-grade surveillance), the Undercity (flooded lower streets, no corporate footprint), and the Sprawl beyond the flood line (tent cities, black-market fabricators, unregistered hab-blocks).

The year is 2087. National governments exist but exert control through corporate charters and regulatory frameworks. The real power belongs to the megacorporations — each one controls an entire sector, employs entire neighborhoods, and answers to no elected body.

## The Megacorps

**Arasaka-Müller** — defense contracting, private military, cyberware. They make the guns and the implants that keep you alive after using them.

**Militech-NOV** — military logistics, drone warfare, security infrastructure. Arasaka-Müller's rival. The cold war between them runs the city's blood.

**Biotechnica** — genetic engineering, life extension, synthetic organs. They own the clinics that keep the augmented alive. They also own the patents on the modifications.

**NBN-Kiwi** — media, data, surveillance. They know what you watched, what you bought, what you thought about. Their news feeds are entertainment; their entertainment is intelligence.

**Kiroshi** — optics, prosthetics, neural interfaces. They make the tech that lets you see through walls and hack through firewalls. Their products are legendary; their countermeasures are worse.

**Ziggurat** — construction, real estate, displacement. They buy the flooded sectors, drain them, sell them back at thirty times the price. The flood is always coming.

## The Economy

The city runs on credsticks and crypto-chits. Corporate payroll hits on the cycle. Street economy runs on barter, favors, and raw data. A corp grunt makes 3,000 credits a cycle. A street doc charges in trade. A fixer takes twenty percent and doesn't ask questions.

Water is filtered but cheap. Food is synthetic but filling. Housing is a lease or a squat. The rent is always due. The corps own the buildings; the gangs own the alleys; the street owns what the street takes.

## Technology

**Cyberware** is common but not universal. Augmentations range from basic — opticks, subdermal chips, neural boosters — to extreme full-body conversions. Rejection syndrome affects unregistered implants. Dependency syndrome makes some drugs necessary for survival with specific augmentations. Black-market cyberware works but carries risk.

**Netrunning** is the digital frontier. The Net is the global data mesh — corporate intranets, public databases, black-market bazaar nodes. ICE (intrusion countermeasures) protects corporate data. Breaching ICE is dangerous; counter-ICE is rarer and deadlier.

**Weapons** range from kinetic pistols and smart-rifles to monomolecular blades and drone-warheads. Ammunition is specific to caliber. Corporate weapons are registered; street weapons are not.

## What Still Works

The maglev system — limited routes, heavy surveillance. The water filtration plants. The black-market fabricators. The independent clinics that don't ask for ID. The old fiber-optic backbone beneath the city. The human instinct to survive.

## The Street

Gang territory is drawn in murals and gunfire. The Voodoo Boys control the data-churches. The Maelstromers sell chrome that eats its users. The afterlife clubs are neutral ground — corps and street share a drink there, until they don't.

Informants are currency. Fixers broker everything from data-drops to body disposal. Netrunners sell access; street samurai sell force; corpo defectors sell secrets.

The police respond to corp disputes. They ignore the Undercity. The line between security force and mercenary is a billing rate.

## What's Breaking

The flood barriers are failing. Sector 7G was evacuated last cycle — no one knows where they went. The Net is getting more hostile; ICE patterns are evolving faster than counter-ICE can track. Corp contracts are getting riskier; the payout is higher but the kill-ratio is higher too.

The city is drowning slowly. The corps are fighting a war neither side will admit. And the people in between — augmented and unmodified alike — are just trying to stay dry.
