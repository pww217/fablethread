# The Expanse — Belter World Bible

## Canon facts (non-negotiable)

- Year: 2351. Humanity has colonized Earth, Mars, and the asteroid Belt. The protomolecule and Eros incident are not yet public knowledge.
- Earth is governed by the United Nations (UN). Mars is governed by the Martian Congressional Republic (MCRN). The Belt has no single government; the Outer Planets Alliance (OPA) is the closest thing, but it is fragmented into competing factions with different interests.
- Belters are physically distinct — tall, thin, elongated from low-gravity development across generations. Earth and Mars consider them second-class citizens. Belters consider the Belt their home and the Inners their exploiters.
- Water, air, and reaction mass are the three currencies that matter most in the Belt. Inner-system corporations — primarily Protogen, Anderson Dawes' OPA faction, and Earth-backed resource companies — control supply chains.
- The Epstein Drive is standard propulsion. Brachistochrone trajectories between major bodies still take days to weeks. High-g burns require acceleration chairs and juice (pharmaceutical g-tolerance compound).
- Standard space combat: PDCs (point-defense cannons), railguns, torpedoes. EVA suits are standard Belter work gear — mag boots, tethers, handheld thrusters.
- No artificial gravity except spin (stations) or thrust (ships under burn). Zero-g aboard unpowered or drifting ships.

## Political landscape (2351)

- The UN and MCRN are nominally at peace but in a cold economic war over Belt resources and shipping lanes.
- Star Helix Security administers law enforcement on Ceres Station under UN contract. In practice the OPA controls the lower levels; Star Helix picks its fights carefully.
- Anderson Dawes leads the largest OPA faction on Ceres — political, patient, dangerous in quiet ways. Other OPA factions operate with more muscle and less strategy.
- Smuggling is the lifeblood of independent Belt freight operators. Legal cargo barely covers fuel and dock fees. The grey market keeps ships flying.

## Economics
- Ceres Station: ~3 million residents. Major Belt hub — trade, gray market, politics, crew hire.
- Dock fees at Ceres run 4,000–6,000 credits per 30-day cradle for a light freighter.
- Common independent cargo: water filtration cores, contraband nutrients, MCRN small arms, prohibited tech, OPA documents, refugees, medical supplies, Martian pharmaceuticals.
- Credits are universal on Ceres and most stations. Barter is common in outer-belt.

## Physics and safety
- Hard physics apply — no FTL, no handwaved gravity.
- Violence is fast, ugly, and permanent. A fight means injuries, debt, or Star Helix attention.
- Ship problems are character problems. A failing water recycler is a deadline, not set dressing.
- Money is tight. Every credit has a name on it.
