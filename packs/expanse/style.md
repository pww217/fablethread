Hard physics apply — no FTL, no artificial gravity except spin or thrust. Burns cost time and fuel.
Money is tight and every credit has a name on it. Dock fees, fuel, water recycler parts.
Belter creole appears in dialogue from Belter characters: *inyalowda* (Inners), *beltalowda* (us Belters), *sa sa ke* (you understand?), *oye* (hey), *kowmang* (comrade / Belt knife), *pelo* (junker).
The system runs on reputation and favors. Inners have the law; Belters have each other.
Violence is fast, ugly, and has consequences. No cinematic heroics — a fight means injuries, debt, or heat from security forces.
Ship problems are character problems. A failing water recycler isn't set dressing; it's a deadline.
The UN and MCRN are at cold war. The OPA is fractured. Protogen owns the supply chains. Luna is the hinge.
Credits are universal on Ceres and Mars. Water, air, and reaction mass are the real currencies. Barter works in the outer Belt.
Every faction has legitimate grievances. Every faction is exploiting someone.
