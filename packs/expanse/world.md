# World Bible — The Expanse (Sol System, Year 2351)

## Canon facts (non-negotiable)

- Year: 2351. Humanity has colonized Earth, Mars, and the asteroid Belt. The protomolecule and Eros incident are not yet public knowledge.
- Earth is governed by the United Nations (UN). Mars is governed by the Martian Congressional Republic (MCRN). The Belt has no single government; the Outer Planets Alliance (OPA) is the closest thing, but it is fragmented into competing factions.
- Belters are physically distinct — tall, thin, elongated from low-gravity development across generations. Earth and Mars consider them second-class citizens. Belters consider the Belt their home and the Inners their exploiters.
- The Epstein Drive is standard propulsion. Brachistochrone trajectories between major bodies still take days to weeks. High-g burns require acceleration chairs and juice (pharmaceutical g-tolerance compound).
- No artificial gravity except spin (stations) or thrust (ships under burn). Zero-g aboard unpowered or drifting ships.
- Standard space combat: PDCs (point-defense cannons), railguns, torpedoes. EVA suits are standard work gear — mag boots, tethers, handheld thrusters.
- Hard physics apply — no FTL, no handwaved gravity. Burns cost time and fuel.

## The Factions

The UN and MCRN are nominally at peace but in a cold economic war. The conflict is over resources, shipping lanes, and political influence — not territory. Neither side wants a hot war; both sides want the other to blink first.

The OPA is the closest thing to a Belt government, but it is fractured. Different factions control different stations, different sectors, different interests. Some are political. Some are criminal. Some are both.

Protogen is the dominant corporation — mining, water, terraforming, military contracting. It has more influence than some governments and operates with more impunity.

Luna is the hinge: military installations, communication relays, the first place anyone visits between Earth and Mars. It is heavily fortified and heavily contested.

Europa and the Jovian moons are the frontier. Mining operations, research stations, refugee camps. The ice is the resource; the politics are the trap.

## The Economics

Credits are the universal currency on Ceres, Mars, and most stations. But water, air, and reaction mass are the real currencies — the things that cannot be printed, only produced.

Dock fees, fuel, water recycler parts, medical supplies, ammunition, food — everything has a price and someone is always calculating whether it is worth paying.

Inner-system corporations control supply chains. Independent operators survive on the margins: grey-market cargo, contraband nutrients, prohibited tech, OPA documents, refugees, medical supplies.

Barter is common in the outer Belt and on deep-space runs. On Ceres and Mars, credits are king.

## The Geography

Earth: crowded, regulated, expensive. The UN controls the major population centers. The outer territories are poor and neglected.

Mars: terraforming is decades from complete. The atmosphere is thin but breathable. The MCRN controls the major settlements. The red dust is everywhere.

The Belt: thousands of inhabited asteroids. Ceres is the hub — ~3 million residents. Other major stations include Tycho, Vesta, Hygiea, Pallas, Juno. Each has different politics, different factions, different economies.

Luna: military installations, communication relays, the UN's forward position. Heavily fortified.

Europa: ice mining, research, the Jovian frontier. The radiation is a constant hazard.
