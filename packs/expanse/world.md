# World Bible — The Expanse (Sol System, Year 2351)

Humanity has colonized Earth (UN), Mars (MCRN), and the Belt (OPA — fragmented, no single government). Belters are physically distinct — tall, thin, elongated from generations in low gravity — considered second-class by Inners, considering the Belt their home. The Epstein Drive is standard propulsion; hard physics apply, no FTL.

The UN and MCRN are in a cold economic war over resources and shipping lanes; neither wants hot war, both want the other to blink. The OPA is fractured into competing factions — political, criminal, both. Protogen dominates mining, water, terraforming, and military contracting with more influence than some governments.
