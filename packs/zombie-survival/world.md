# World Bible — H7N9-X Outbreak

H7N9-X is a neurotropic influenza variant: 4–18 hour incubation, indistinguishable from flu, and full neurological reanimation within 2–6 hours of cardiac arrest — the body rises regardless of cause of death if not destroyed at the brainstem. Infected track movement, noise, and smell; they do not coordinate or plan.

Six months in, national governments have collapsed or gone silent. Power grids failed in the first eight weeks. No external rescue is coming. Pockets of military and paramilitary groups hold territory under their own rules — some protect civilians, some prey on them.
