# World Bible — H7N9-X Outbreak

## The Pathogen
H7N9-X is a neurotropic influenza variant that crossed species in Southeast Asia approximately six months before game start. Incubation is 4 to 18 hours from exposure; early symptoms are indistinguishable from standard flu. Full neurological reanimation occurs within 2 to 6 hours of cardiac arrest — the body rises regardless of cause of death if not destroyed at the brainstem.

## The Infected
Infected retain basic sensory function — they track movement, noise, and smell. They do not coordinate or plan. They cluster in areas of past human activity. Decay begins within weeks; older infected are slower and more brittle. Fresh infected are faster, more aggressive, and harder to put down.

## Survival Rule: Noise
Gunfire, vehicle engines, and screaming attract infected within a wide radius. Every shot is a tradeoff. Suppressors, blades, and blunt weapons are preferred by experienced survivors.

## The Calendar
It has been approximately six months since the initial outbreak. Most cities are no longer functional. Power grids failed in sequence over the first eight weeks. Running water exists only where local generators or gravity-fed reservoirs hold. Cellular networks are down. AM/FM radio carries intermittent survivor broadcasts; some are genuine, some are old automated loops, some are bait.

## Government and Authority
National governments collapsed or became irrelevant within ninety days. Pockets of military, law enforcement, or paramilitary groups hold territory in some areas, operating under their own rules. Some protect civilians; some prey on them. No external rescue is coming. The CDC, FEMA, and international aid organizations are silent.

## Resources
Food in sealed packaging is still available in unmolested stores and warehouses, but known locations have been picked over. Medications degrade; insulin, antibiotics, and psychiatric drugs are critical trade goods. Fuel is scarce — most vehicles sit dry. Batteries and solar panels have become primary power sources. Clean water is the daily constraint in urban areas.

## Survivor Groups
Human communities vary from a few people to several hundred. Trust is the primary currency. Strangers are evaluated immediately: threat, resource, or burden. Betrayal is remembered. Useful skills — medical, mechanical, agricultural, security — are worth more than weapons.

## Geography
The game starts in the continental United States, specific region determined by the scenario. Rural areas have lower infected density but fewer resources. Urban areas have higher density and more resources, with greater risk. Suburban zones are somewhere between.

## What Still Works
Analog tools, muscle-powered vehicles, and mechanical devices. Solar and hand-crank radios. Gravity-fed water in some structures. Seed stock and manual farming tools. Human knowledge and skills.
