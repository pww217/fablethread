
## Turn 0 — Seed

The rhythmic clatter of a typewriter with a stuck 'e' key punctuates the heavy silence of the Fourth Precinct. Rainwater drips from your trench coat onto the floor, adding to the slick, dark patches reflecting the dim overhead lights. The air in the room is thick, a suffocating mix of stale coffee and the blue smoke hanging under the ceiling lamps like a low-lying fog.

You sit at a scarred wooden desk, the stained page of the union ledger feeling heavy and illicit against your palm. Across the room, Andrew Vance is hunched over his station, his fingers moving mechanically through a stack of summonses, his face a mask of weary indifference to the chaos of the city outside. The precinct feels unusually quiet, yet there is an underlying vibration of tension that makes the hair on your neck stand up.

A heavy footfall breaks the stillness. Detective Sean Moran rounds the corner, his eyes immediately locking onto yours with a sharp, predatory stillness. He doesn't walk toward you so much as he closes the distance, his presence cutting through the haze of smoke like a blade. He stops just short of your desk, leaning forward so that the light catches the hardness in his gaze.

'You shouldn't be digging in those files, Halloway,' Moran says, his voice low and devoid of warmth. He doesn't look at the ledger, but his eyes scan your face for any sign of hesitation. 'Some things in this precinct are better left in the bottom drawer.' Behind him, Vance stops typing, the sudden silence of the machine feeling louder than the noise ever did. Moran reaches out, resting a heavy hand on the edge of your desk, his knuckles white. He isn't making a threat yet, but the implication hangs in the air between you, more potent than any spoken warning. You realize then that the discrepancy you found in the payroll wasn't just a mistake—it was a breadcrumb leading straight into a trap.


## Turn 1 — Examine the stained pay ledger for discrepancies in the fund entries.

You pull the *Stained Pay Ledger* closer, ignoring Moran’s heavy presence and his warning to stay away from the files. You pick up the *Brass Magnifying Loupe* and lean over the torn page, squinting through the lens to isolate the ink strokes of the handwritten figures.

The discrepancies emerge clearly under magnification. While most entries follow a standard pattern of deduction for union dues and transit fees, three specific lines in the dockworkers' payroll show irregularities where the ink density suggests later alterations or erasures. The numbers have been carefully overwritten to balance a total that doesn't match the individual worker contributions listed above them.

Moran’s knuckles remain white against your desk, his shadow falling over the document and obscuring your view. He doesn't move to grab the page, but he stays rooted in place, watching your eyes track the ink. Behind you, Andrew Vance remains motionless at his station, his gaze fixed on a stack of summonses, though his rhythmic typing has not resumed.

You slide the *Stained Pay Ledger* shut and stand to face Sean Moran directly, bringing your eyes level with his. The heavy shadow that had been obscuring your work lifts, leaving the desk in a dim, uneven light.

Moran does not recoil or step back; instead, he leans forward slightly, closing the distance between you until his face is inches from yours. His expression remains a mask of stony indifference, but his eyes narrow, tracking the movement of your jaw.

"You're digging in shallow soil, Halloway," Moran says, his voice dropping to a low, transactional rasp that barely carries over the quiet hum of the precinct. "And you're going to find it’s much harder to climb out once you hit the bedrock."

He reaches into his breast pocket and pulls out a small, unlit cigarette, tapping it against the edge of your desk with a slow, rhythmic thud. He doesn't light it, but he holds it between two fingers like a silent ultimatum, waiting to see if you will blink first. Behind him, Andrew Vance finally looks up from his summonses, his eyes darting between you and the Sergeant before he quickly returns to his paperwork.

You turn away from his stare and walk past Andrew Vance, who keeps his head bowed low over his summonses, though his pen stops moving the moment your shadow falls across his desk. You navigate through the rows of scarred wooden desks and move toward the heavy door leading to the stairwell at the rear of the precinct.

The air cools as you descend the narrow stone steps, leaving the tobacco smoke and dim overheads behind for a cramped, lightless descent. The basement smells of damp concrete and stagnant water. At the bottom of the stairs, a single low-wattage bulb hangs from a frayed wire, casting a sickly yellow circle over a row of heavy metal filing cabinets and several stacked wooden crates. One cabinet door stands slightly ajar, its lock mangled as if forced by a heavy tool.

A heavy footfall echoes from the top of the stone stairs, vibrating through the damp concrete floor. One shadow stretches long and thin across the wall behind you, growing larger as someone descends into the light.

"Looking for something, Halloway?"
