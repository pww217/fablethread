
## Turn 0 — Seed

The smell of hot tar rises from the blistering roof, thick and suffocating under the midday sun. You steady your breathing, feeling the grit of industrial soot beneath your palms as you crouch near the edge of the Spearsport warehouse. Below, the distant wail of a police siren cuts through the heavy air, a lonely sound that underscores the silence of this high-altitude wasteland. A loose antenna clatters violently in a sudden gust of wind, sounding like a frantic heartbeat against the metal housing.

You aren't supposed to be up here. As a union investigator, your jurisdiction ends at the street level or inside the union halls, but the information Clark Harris whispered to you before he vanished changed everything. He didn't just hand you a lead; he handed you a death sentence wrapped in newsprint. Now, looking across the gap between this building and the next, the distance looks much wider than it did from the street. The fire escape on the adjacent structure ends abruptly six feet short of your current position, a jagged iron limb reaching for a ledge it will never touch.

A frantic scuffle breaks out near the water tower. Clark Harris bursts through the heavy metal door leading to the roof, his eyes wide and wild, clutching a manila envelope to his chest like it was a holy relic. He looks less like a journalist and more like a cornered animal. Behind him, Thomas Vance emerges, his massive frame silhouetted against the bright sky. Vance isn't running; he is stalking, his heavy boots thudding rhythmically on the tarred surface. His face is set in a grim mask of duty, though his eyes flicker with a frantic energy that contradicts his steady pace.

"Give it here, Clark!" Vance bellows, his voice cutting through the wind. "The union can't have this mess turning into a circus! We handle this internally!"

Clark doesn't stop. He lunges toward your position, his eyes locking onto yours for a split second—a look of desperate recognition and terror. He knows you are the only one who can read what is inside that envelope without selling it to the highest bidder or burying it in a headline. He trips on a loose patch of roofing material, the envelope slipping from his grasp and sliding toward the edge of the roof. At the same time, Vance reaches for his heavy coat pocket, his hand closing around something substantial and metallic. The gap between you and Clark is growing, and the distance to the next building feels like an abyss. You have seconds to decide if you are going to protect the truth or let it fall into the shadows of the docks below.


## Turn 1 — I head toward the Gazette building to find where the leak originated.

You lunge from your crouch and sprint toward the edge of the roof, eyes fixed on the distant silhouette of the *Spearsport Gazette* building looming over the city skyline. Your boots skid on the loose granules of tar as you aim for the gap between buildings, intending to bypass this confrontation and find the source of Clark Harris's frantic leak.

The transition is impossible; your stride carries you straight into empty air where the fire escape ends abruptly six feet short of the ledge. You stumble, hands slapping fruitlessly against the grit-covered roof as gravity yanks at your center of gravity. The manila envelope skitters past your fingertips, sliding toward the precipice and dangling precariously over the sheer drop to the docks below.

"Get back from the edge!" Thomas Vance roars, his heavy boots thudding closer as he closes the distance between you and the corner. He ignores your sudden movement toward the street, his focus entirely on intercepting Clark Harris.

Clark Harris scrambles upward from his fall, his breathing coming in ragged, shallow gasps. He clutches the manila envelope against his chest with trembling hands, his eyes darting between you and the approaching foreman. He doesn't follow your lead toward the city; instead, he recoils further into the center of the roof, trapped between your frantic dash and Vance’s steady advance.


## Turn 2 — I lunge back toward the ledge to snatch the manila envelope before it falls.

You lunge back toward the ledge, fingers clawing through the grit and loose tar in a desperate attempt to snag the manila envelope. Your hand brushes the rough paper for a fraction of a second before your footing gives way on the crumbling roof surface. Instead of catching the prize, your weight carries you forward, and your outstretched arm sweeps the envelope clean off the precipice.

The manila envelope tumbles silently into the dark void between the buildings. You watch it fall, a pale streak against the soot-stained brickwork, until it vanishes into the shadows of the docks below.

"It's gone," Clark Harris whimpers, his voice cracking as he stares at the empty space where the leak had rested moments before. He collapses onto his knees, hands hovering uselessly over his chest.

Thomas Vance reaches the corner where you stumbled and plants his heavy boots firmly on the roof. He looms over you, his shadow stretching long and dark across your prone form. "You're a clumsy fool, Brandon," Vance growls, his eyes narrowing as he steps between you and the frantic reporter. "Now we all have nothing to show for this mess but a trip to the precinct."
