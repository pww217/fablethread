
## Turn 0 — Seed

The dry scrape of windblown sand against your boots is the only constant in the heat. You stand on the edge of the Bentleyport outpost, watching the heat shimmer distorting the horizon into a dancing, liquid mess. A rusted hull fragment lies half buried in the dust nearby, a relic of a freighter that died long before you took this badge. Beside it, a cracked oxygen mask hangs from a post, its filter clogged with fine red silt. The creak of metal expanding in the blistering noon sun sounds like a slow, rhythmic groan from a dying beast.

You see a trail marker knocked askew, pointing toward the canyon floor instead of the settlement gates. It is a small sign of the chaos that has been creeping into the outpost lately. The economic rot has made people desperate, and desperation makes them reckless. You can feel the weight of your sidearm against your hip, a heavy reminder of a duty that feels increasingly hollow in a town that has forgotten how to thrive.

The silence is broken by a sudden, sharp crack—not the metal expanding, but a gunshot echoing from the canyon mouth. Then comes the shouting. James Luna stumbles into view, his clothes torn and his breathing shallow through a cracked respirator. He is sprinting toward your position, eyes wide with a terror that transcends mere exhaustion. Behind him, three militia guards emerge from behind a corrugated metal shed, their rifles raised but their movements hesitant. They aren't looking at the fugitive; they are looking at the shadows moving in the dust behind him.

Raymond Miles steps out from the shade of a nearby hab-unit, his eyes narrowing as he spots you. He doesn't reach for a weapon; instead, he leans against a rusted support beam, watching the scene with a calculating gaze that says he has already weighed the cost of intervention. "He's bringing trouble to our doorstep, Deputy," Raymond calls out, his voice cutting through the dry wind. "And the ones following him don't look like they're here for a chat."

James collapses a few feet from you, clutching a small, glowing data shard to his chest as if it were a holy relic. The militia guards freeze, caught between their orders to arrest the fugitive and the sudden movement in the canyon that suggests a larger force is approaching. The settlement's perimeter is thin, the people are hungry, and the peace you have worked so hard to maintain is fraying at the seams right before your eyes.


## Turn 1 — I check the perimeter sensors in my logbook to see where the scavenger activity is highest.

You flip open your *Logbook* and scan the handwritten notes detailing the latest sensor pings from the settlement's perimeter. The data in your journal shows a cluster of irregular disturbances concentrated near the western canyon mouth, far closer than any sanctioned patrol should allow.

Three militia guards shift their weight, their boots scuffing against the parched earth as they stare toward the dust clouds rising behind James Luna. Raymond Miles remains leaned against the rusted support beam, his eyes darting between you and the approaching movement in the canyon. James Luna lies curled on the ground, his fingers white-knuckled around the glowing data shard while he gasps for air through a failing respirator.


## Turn 2 — I check James Luna's vitals and secure the data shard before turning to the militia guards.

You reach down to check James Luna's vitals, but your fingers find only a shallow, irregular pulse beneath his sweat-slicked skin. As you attempt to pry the glowing data shard from his white-knuckled grip, he jerks violently away, a choked gasp escaping his respirator as he pulls the device closer to his chest in a reflexive spasm of terror.

The three militia guards snap their attention from the canyon toward you, their rifles dipping slightly as they watch your struggle with the fugitive. One guard, a woman named Elena Vance, steps forward and rests a hand on her holster. "Lynch, leave him be," she calls out over the whistling wind. "We aren't here to play medic; we're here to secure the perimeter before that dust cloud hits us."

Raymond Miles pushes off from the rusted support beam, his eyes fixed on the billowing silt in the west. "They're closer than they look," he mutters, his voice cutting through the tension. The dust clouds thicken, and the scuffing sounds of boots become a heavy, synchronized thudding coming from the canyon mouth.


## Turn 3 — I holster my sidearm and turn toward Elena Vance, demanding the data shard from Luna while gesturing toward the approaching dust cloud.

You holster your Service Sidearm and turn toward Elena Vance, gesturing sharply at the approaching dust cloud while demanding that James Luna surrender the data shard. Elena Vance doesn't move her hand from her holster; instead, she steps closer, her eyes darting between you and the heavy thudding sounds growing louder in the haze.

"You want the shard?" Elena asks, her voice flat despite the rising wind. "Then you take responsibility for him. If we bring a fugitive into the outpost while those scavengers are at the gates, it's your neck on the line when the perimeter fails." She looks to the two militia guards, who shift their weight, their rifles held tight against their chests.

Raymond Miles scoffs from the side, his eyes never leaving the western canyon mouth. "You can argue over a piece of tech all you want, but those boots aren't slowing down for a debate."

The synchronized thudding suddenly breaks into a frantic, uneven scramble as the first silhouette emerges from the silt cloud. A scavenger lunges from the haze, a jagged piece of scrap metal raised high. Elena Vance shouts a command to the two militia guards, who immediately pivot their rifles toward the canyon mouth as the dust swallows the approaching threat.


## Turn 4 — Draw my Service Sidearm and aim at the lunging scavenger.

You draw your Service Sidearm and aim at the lunging scavenger, but the thick silt cloud obscures your line of sight just as you find your mark. The scavenger’s jagged scrap metal strikes a rusted support beam inches from your head with a deafening clang, sending a spray of orange flakes into your eyes. Before you can squeeze the trigger, a second scavenger bursts through the haze behind the first, forcing the two militia guards to pivot their rifles and open fire blindly into the dust.

The heavy crack of the guards' rifles echoes against the creaking metal structures as they discharge their rounds into the silt. One scavenger screams and collapses into a heap of twitching limbs, but the second figure continues its frantic charge, fueled by desperation. Elena Vance shouts over the roar of the wind, her voice cutting through the chaos.

"Lynch! They're coming in waves!" 

Raymond Miles ducks behind a heavy crate, his eyes wide as he watches the western canyon mouth swallow more silhouettes. Three more scavengers emerge from the haze, their forms ghostly and indistinct in the choking dust.
