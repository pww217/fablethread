
## Turn 0 — Seed

The smell of stale rye and cheap tobacco clings to the back of your throat. In the corner of The Blind Pig, a phonograph skips on a scratched record, the rhythmic clicking providing a jagged heartbeat to the low laughter drifting from the back room. You sit at a corner table, eyes tracing the worn brass rail where decades of desperate hands have smoothed the metal. The air is thick with a haze of blue cigarette smoke that obscures the faces of the men huddled near the bar.

A sudden silence falls over the room as Joseph Vance steps through the heavy curtained entrance. He does not look like a man on official business; his coat is rumpled and his eyes are darting toward the exit. Behind him, Patrick O'Malley follows, his face a mask of grim tension. They do not head for the bar. Instead, they move directly toward your table, their shadows stretching long across the floorboards under the dim amber lamps.

Vance drops a heavy manila envelope onto your table, right next to your half-empty glass. The clink of his heavy ring against the wood sounds like a gavel. 'The treasurer was found in the alley behind the laundromat,' Vance says, his voice low and raspy. 'No eyes on him, no witnesses, just a hole in his chest and this.' He taps the envelope. Patrick O'Malley leans over, his large hands gripping the edge of your table so hard his knuckles turn white. 'The South Robert boys are already claiming it was a territory warning,' Patrick growls, his eyes searching yours for a glimmer of the man you used to be. 'If this is a union matter, Ben, you need to handle it before the precinct tries to bury it.' The tension in the room is palpable; even the bartender has stopped wiping the counter, frozen by the sight of two men bringing death into a sanctuary of shadows.
