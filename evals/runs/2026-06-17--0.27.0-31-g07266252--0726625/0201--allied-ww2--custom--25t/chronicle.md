"Cook is trying to reach the destroyer off the coast. They need coordinates for a pre-emptive strike on the ridge, but we are running dangerously low on the heavy shells they want us to spot for."

Andrew Cook looks up, his expression more anxious than relieved. "The signal is weak, Sergeant. If I can't get a clear line to the ship, any coordination will be guesswork. And if we use our remaining flares to mark the target, we won't have anything left for an emergency extraction."

Vance turns back to you, his jaw tight. "The Lieutenant is breathing down my neck for those coordinates. He wants the naval guns to clear that ridge before dawn.
