
## Turn 0 — Seed

The acrid smell of bleach concentrate stings your nostrils, thick enough to taste on the back of your tongue. You stand in the shadow of a decontamination tent, its heavy plastic flap torn and flapping rhythmically against the metal frame like a dying heartbeat. Overhead, the rhythmic, distorted loop of a recorded warning echoes endlessly off the cracked concrete walls: 'Remain stationary. Compliance is safety. Awaiting clearance.' The sound is a dull, maddening drone that never quite fills the silence between its own repetitions.

You glance toward the perimeter fence, where layers of yellow biohazard tape flutter in the biting wind, looking like strips of dead skin clinging to the wire. Nearby, a pile of bedding has been abandoned in the mud, soaked through and smelling of damp rot, suggesting someone left in a desperate hurry. The sector is quiet, save for the loudspeaker's mechanical drone, but the tension is thick, heavy as the humidity before a storm.

You are not supposed to be here. As a pariah, your presence in the quarantine zone is a violation of the Ministry's latest decree, yet the water canisters you carry are the only thing keeping the people in the lower slums from total dehydration. You feel the weight of the steel wrench in your pocket, a cold reminder of the world that used to work.

The sound of heavy boots crunching on gravel breaks the loop of the loudspeaker. Two Enclave guards emerge from the haze of the decontamination corridor, their silhouettes sharp against the grey light. The taller one, whose eyes dart nervously toward the torn tent, holds his rifle at a low ready, while the shorter guard keeps his gaze fixed strictly on the ground, his jaw tight.

Then, the third figure approaches, walking with a stride that commands the space between the ruins. Nicholas Shaw. He doesn't look like the man who once shared a ration of real coffee with you in the dark of the old barracks; he looks like a machine of the Ministry now, his uniform pressed and his expression unreadable. He stops ten feet from you, the guards flanking him like twin pillars of the law.

'Terrance,' Nicholas says, his voice low, barely rising above the drone of the loudspeaker. He doesn't draw his weapon, but his hand rests heavily on the grip of his sidearm. 'You were seen near the supply depot. The Ministry does not take kindly to unauthorized inventory inspections.'

The guards shift, their eyes locking onto yours. You can see the sweat bead on the tall guard's brow despite the chill. They are terrified, and that makes them dangerous. Nicholas steps closer, his eyes searching yours for a sign of the man he used to know, or perhaps looking for the moment he has to justify the order to detain you. The reality of the rationing crisis is written in the hollows of his cheeks, a truth he is paid to deny, even as the people starve just beyond the fence.


## Turn 1 — Sneak into the Ministry's central archives.

You turn to slip away, eyes darting toward the narrow gap between the decontamination tent and the industrial ruins, intending to vanish into the haze before they can react. You take a sudden, sharp step toward the shadows, but the heavy crunch of gravel under your boots betrays your movement immediately.

"Don't even think about it," Nicholas Shaw commands, his voice snapping like a whip across the desolate concrete. He takes a decisive stride forward, cutting off your path to the ruins and forcing you back toward the center of the corridor. 

The tall guard with the nervous eye twitches, his rifle rising from its low-ready position to level directly at your chest, the barrel trembling slightly. The short, stout guard follows suit, stepping to your flank to seal the exit, their jaw set in a grim, practiced line. You are boxed in between the Enclave's steel and the rotting tarp of the tent, with nowhere to run and no cover to exploit.


## Turn 2 — I reach for the heavy steel wrench at my belt, bracing myself to swing if they close the distance.

You reach for the Heavy Steel Wrench at your belt, your fingers curling around the cold, oxidized metal to brace for a strike. The sudden movement causes the tall guard with the nervous eye to jerk, his finger tightening visibly against the trigger of his rifle. 

Nicholas Shaw takes a heavy stride forward, closing the gap until he is inches from you, his Enclave uniform casting a shadow over your hands. He stares down at the wrench, then meets your eyes with a gaze that flickers between professional coldness and a desperate, hidden recognition.

"Drop it, Terrance," Nicholas Shaw says, his voice dropping to a low, strained hiss that barely carries past your ears. "Don't make me do this."


## Turn 3 — I lower the wrench slowly, keeping my eyes locked on his, and whisper, "Then don't."

You release your grip on the Heavy Steel Wrench, letting the metal clatter heavily against the cracked concrete between you and Nicholas Shaw. "Then don't," you whisper, holding his gaze with a defiance that offers no room for negotiation.

Nicholas Shaw flinches as if the words were a physical blow, his jaw tightening so hard the muscle pulses beneath his skin. The tall Enclave Guard with the nervous eye loses his composure; his rifle barrel wobbles violently as he fights to keep the sight leveled at your chest, his breathing coming in shallow, ragged bursts.

"I can't," Nicholas Shaw says, his voice cracking, abandoning the hiss for a raw, terrified plea. 

Before he can finish the thought, the guard's finger twitches against the trigger. A single shot cracks through the desolate air of the quarantine zone, the sound echoing sharply off the industrial ruins. The round misses your head by inches, instead slamming into the Heavy Steel Wrench you just dropped. The impact sends a spray of oxidized metal shards across the ground, and the wrench skitters away, lost beneath a heavy tarp near the perimeter fence.


## Turn 4 — Dive for cover behind the nearest concrete pillar.

You dive toward the nearest concrete pillar, seeking the safety of its thick, weathered base, but your boots skid on the loose grit and metal shards left by the shot. Before you can press your body against the stone, a second crack erupts from the guard's rifle. The round strikes the edge of the pillar just above your head, showering your hair and shoulders with stinging-hot concrete dust and jagged splinters of masonry. 

The tall Enclave Guard with the nervous eye lets out a choked, panicked sob, his rifle bucking again as he pulls the trigger in a blind, rhythmic frenzy. Each shot thuds into the pillar or the dirt, coming too fast to track, turning your attempted cover into a deathtrap of flying debris. Nicholas Shaw lunges forward, grabbing the guard by the shoulder and wrenching the weapon upward toward the sky to break the uncontrolled spray of fire. 

"Cease fire! Stand down!" Nicholas Shaw screams, his voice cracking with a desperate, frantic authority that fails to mask the terror in his eyes. The guard's rifle continues to discharge sporadically into the air, the muzzle flashes bright and ugly against the gray industrial ruins, while Nicholas Shaw stares down at you from across the gap, his face pale and sweating despite the chill of the quarantine zone.


## Turn 5 — I scramble behind the pillar, pressing my back firmly against the cold concrete to shield myself from the spray of debris.

You scramble behind the pillar, pressing your back firmly against the cold concrete to shield yourself from the spray of debris. The impact of the masonry stings your skin, and the sudden, rhythmic thudding of the Enclave Guard's rifle leaves your head ringing and your vision swimming in a haze of gray dust. 

One of the guard's stray rounds strikes a tarp-covered crate resting just feet away from your cover. The impact ignites a secondary explosion of splintering wood and heavy industrial plastic, sending a fresh cascade of jagged fragments and thick, choking grit raining down upon you. You huddle lower, shielding your face as the debris clatters against the pillar's surface like hail on a tin roof.

Across the gap, Nicholas Shaw continues to wrestle the tall Enclave Guard, whose left eye twitches violently as he fights to regain control of his weapon. The guard's heavy chest plate scrapes loudly against the Enclave Captain's uniform as they struggle in the dirt, both men momentarily distracted by the uncontrolled discharge of fire into the sky.


## Turn 6 — I creep toward the fallen crate to see if anything useful survived the blast.

You attempt to crawl through the thick, choking grit toward the splintered crate, but your limbs feel heavy and uncoordinated as the ringing in your ears refuses to subside. Each movement sends fresh waves of vertigo through your vision, making the ground appear to tilt and sway beneath your hands. Before you can even reach the edge of the debris, the sound of heavy, rhythmic footsteps thunders across the concrete, coming from the direction of the struggle between Nicholas Shaw and the tall Enclave Guard.

The scene shifts as you are forced to scramble away from the wreckage to avoid being seen, stumbling blindly through the haze of dust and toward the shadows of the industrial ruins. You stumble into a narrow, lightless alleyway between two corrugated metal warehouses, the sound of the Enclave's shouting fading into a dull roar behind you. The air here is stagnant, thick with the smell of damp rust and rotting tarp, providing a sudden, heavy silence that contrasts sharply with the chaos of the quarantine zone.


## Turn 7 — Search the shadows of the alleyway for a vantage point to observe the struggle.

You press your palms against the cold, damp corrugated metal to steady yourself, squinting through the haze of dust to find a gap between the warehouses that offers a view of the quarantine zone. The vertigo pulls at your vision, making the dark entrance to the alleyway seem to stretch and warp as you try to focus on the sounds coming from the street. 

The rhythmic thud of boots continues, punctuated by the sharp, irregular clatter of dropped equipment and the strained, hushed tones of Nicholas Shaw's commands. A sudden, heavy metallic clang echoes through the passage, followed by the sound of a body hitting the ground hard. The silence of the alleyway holds, thick and suffocating, as you wait for the shouting to escalate or for the footsteps to approach your position.


## Turn 8 — Crouch low behind the corrugated metal and peer through the gap to identify who was dropped and where the guards are moving.

You press your belly against the damp, rusted surface of the corrugated metal, lowering your center of gravity to minimize your profile. Through the narrow gap between the warehouses, you squint past the swirling grit to survey the street's sudden commotion.

One Enclave Guard—the tall man with the nervous twitch in his left eye—lies sprawled face-down on the cobblestones, his heavily reinforced chest plate reflecting the dull light of the quarantine zone. Nicholas Shaw stands over him, his posture rigid despite the chaos, gesturing sharply toward the fallen man's dropped rifle. The second Enclave Guard remains several meters away, pacing in a tight, frantic circle while gripping his weapon with white-knuckled intensity. The two guards are focused entirely on their fallen comrade, their backs turned to the dark mouth of your alleyway.


## Turn 9 — Creep toward the fallen guard's dropped rifle to scavenge it or use it as a distraction.

You belly-crawl across the grit, moving with a rhythmic, low-profile slide that keeps your silhouette lost among the shadows of the corrugated metal. The ringing in your ears begins to subside, replaced by the frantic, shallow breathing of the pacing guard and the sharp, clipped commands coming from Nicholas Shaw. You reach the edge of the light, stretching a hand out to snag the fallen Enclave Guard's rifle just as it slides a few inches further into the muck. Your fingers lock around the cold, textured grip, pulling the weapon silently into the darkness of the alleyway before the pacing guard can complete his frantic circuit. The rifle is heavy and still warm from the previous discharge, its barrel smelling of burnt propellant.


## Turn 10 — I slip further into the shadows to inspect the rifle's magazine and check for any nearby security cameras or light sweeps.

You press your back against the cold, corrugated metal of the warehouse, pulling the Enclave Guard Rifle tight against your chest to keep it out of the light. You work the release on the magazine well, sliding the heavy, rectangular block free to inspect the brass-cased rounds seated inside; the ammunition is standard Ministry issue, clean and unblemished despite the filth of the alleyway. Shifting your gaze upward, you scan the rim of the dark passage for the telltale red blink of a surveillance lens or the sweeping arc of a patrol light, but the shadows remain unbroken by electronic eyes. The only movement comes from the pacing guard, whose boots crunch rhythmically on the grit just beyond your cover, and the low, strained murmur of Nicholas Shaw's voice as he attempts to regain control of the situation.
