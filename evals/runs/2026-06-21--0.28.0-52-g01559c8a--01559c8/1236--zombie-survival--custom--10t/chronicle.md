
## Turn 0 — Seed

The rhythmic, metallic drip of condensation from an overhead pipe hits the concrete floor with a hollow, lonely sound. You can taste the stale, copper-heavy air of the bunker, a scent that has become more familiar to you than the smell of rain ever was. In the dim light of the substation, the water filtration pump groans, its cracked housing weeping a steady stream of greyish fluid onto the floor.

You are hunched over the control panel, your fingers tracing the jagged fracture in the casing. Beside you, the heavy steel wrench feels like an extension of your own arm, a tool that has seen more of the post-collapse world than most of the people living above this bunker ever will. The military radio on the workbench beside you emits nothing but low, rhythmic static, a white noise that fills the gaps between the pump's dying gasps.

The heavy steel door at the top of the stairs thuds shut, the sound echoing sharply down the narrow corridor. You don't need to look up to know who it is; the heavy, measured footsteps of Melvin Reeves carry a weight that isn't just physical. He approaches, his shadow stretching long and thin across the damp floor, accompanied by the rhythmic clatter of two militia guards. 

Melvin stops just short of your workspace, his presence casting a chill that has nothing to do with the subterranean temperature. He doesn't look at the leak or the cracked housing; he looks directly at you, his eyes hard and unyielding. Behind him, the two guards stand like statues, their hands resting near the holsters of their sidearms. One of them, a man with a nervous twitch in his jaw, keeps glancing toward the copper wire spool sitting on your workbench.

'The Council has made a decision, Jeffery,' Melvin says, his voice devoid of any warmth. He gestures vaguely toward the settlement above, where the water crisis is turning into a riot. 'We cannot afford to waste more of the sector's remaining copper on experimental repairs that might not hold. The militia needs those supplies to reinforce the North Luis perimeter.'

You feel the weight of his gaze, and more importantly, the unspoken threat of the guards behind him. You know what he is really saying. He isn't here to ask for your expertise; he is here to strip you of the very materials you need to prevent the entire system from collapsing. If you use that copper to patch the pump, you leave the perimeter vulnerable to the faction raids that have been increasing near the border closures. If you give it to him, the water stops flowing by morning.

'The pump won't hold another forty-eight hours without those reinforcements,' you say, your voice raspy from the dry air. You keep your eyes on the cracked housing, refusing to meet his stare. 'If I don't use the wire now, there won't be a settlement left to defend.'

Melvin steps closer, his shadow swallowing your work area completely. 'The settlement survives by order, not by hope, Engineer. Hand over the spool. Now.' One of the guards shifts his weight, the leather of his gear creaking in the silence of the bunker. You can feel the tension coiled like a spring between the man who once helped you hold a breach during the Great Flight and the man who now views your survival as an inconvenient variable in his calculations.


## Turn 1 — I head toward the heavy blast doors of the Filtration Substation.

You push away from the control panel, the heavy steel wrench clattering against the workbench as you stand to face Melvin Reeves. You ignore his command, stepping around the leaking pump and moving toward the heavy blast doors of the Filtration Substation.

Melvin's jaw tightens, his shadow flickering against the concrete as he takes a sudden, sharp step forward to intercept you. "I didn't give you leave to walk away, Jeffery," Melvin says, his voice dropping into a low, dangerous register that cuts through the rhythmic dripping of the leak. Behind him, the guard with the nervous jaw unholsters his sidearm halfway, the metal clicking sharply in the quiet chamber.

You ignore them both, pushing through the heavy blast doors and stepping into the narrow, dimly lit corridor that leads away from the pump room. The transition from the humid, copper-scented air of the substation to the dry, biting chill of the access tunnel hits you instantly. Your boots crunch on grit and loose gravel as you move quickly down the passage, leaving the sound of Melvin's heavy footsteps and the guard's frantic breathing behind you.

The corridor opens into a wider junction where the secondary conduits run along the walls like exposed veins. Ahead, the tunnel splits: one path leads upward toward the settlement's main intake, while the other descends deeper into the lower maintenance shafts of North Luis.


## Turn 2 — I head down into the lower maintenance shafts of North Luis.

You pivot toward the downward path, descending into the lower maintenance shafts of North Luis. Your boots scrape against the steep, grit-covered stairs as you push deeper into the darkness of the shaft.

"Melvin, wait! Don't shoot!" The short militia guard with nervous hands screams from the junction behind you, his voice cracking as he draws his sidearm fully from its holster. The frantic metallic slide of his pistol echoes sharply against the tunnel walls, followed by his heavy, stumbling footsteps as he chases after Melvin Reeves.

The descent steepens, forcing you to lean into the cold stone wall to maintain your balance. You move past rusted junction boxes and weeping conduits that groan under the pressure of the water grid above. The light from the upper junction fades quickly, swallowed by the oppressive gloom of the lower shafts. As you reach a wider landing halfway down the shaft, the sounds of the pursuit become muffled by the thick, damp walls, leaving only the distant, hollow drip of condensation hitting the floor.


## Turn 3 — I draw my service pistol and scan the darkness of the landing, listening for Melvin's footsteps.

You draw your Service Pistol, the metal cool and familiar against your palm, and sweep the barrel through the heavy gloom of the landing. You freeze, straining to catch the rhythm of Melvin Reeves' footsteps or the frantic scuffle of the short guard with nervous hands, but all you hear is the steady, rhythmic drip of water from a weeping conduit.

The short guard’s distant, panicked shouting suddenly cuts through the silence of the lower shafts, muffled by the stone walls but carrying an edge of terror that goes beyond his pursuit of you. From the darkness of a side-ventilation duct to your left, a low, wet scraping sound responds to his cries—a heavy, sliding friction of something large moving against metal. The sound isn't coming from the stairs behind you, but from the pitch-black recesses of the landing itself.


## Turn 4 — I aim my service pistol toward the side-ventilation duct and move cautiously toward the edge of the light.

You aim your Service Pistol at the side-ventilation duct and retreat into the edge of the light, pressing your back against a weeping conduit to minimize your silhouette. The scraping sound from within the vent transitions from a slide into a frantic, heavy clawing of metal on metal that echoes through the landing. A massive, pale limb—thick as a man's torso and covered in slick, translucent skin—slams out of the duct opening, shattering the rusted grate.

The creature lunges from the darkness, its weight hitting the stone floor with a wet thud that vibrates through your boots. Before it can fully emerge, you squeeze the trigger of your Service Pistol. The muzzle flash momentarily turns the gloom into a strobe of white light, illuminating a nightmare of elongated limbs and sightless, milky eyes. The bullet strikes the creature's flank, spraying a thick, greyish fluid against the conduit, but the impact only seems to accelerate its frenzy. It lunges forward, driven by a sudden, violent hunger, its heavy body skidding across the damp landing toward your position.


## Turn 5 — I dive to the side and swing the heavy steel wrench at its nearest limb.

You dive to the side, your boots slipping on the damp stone as you swing the Heavy Steel Wrench toward the creature's nearest limb. The heavy tool whistles through the air, but the Pale Creature’s frantic, spasming movement causes it to lurch unpredictably; the wrench connects only with empty air and a spray of greyish fluid. As the creature slams its massive weight into the wall to stabilize itself, its thick torso crushes a weeping conduit. A seam in the metal splits wide, and a violent jet of scalding steam erupts from the rupture, hissing loudly as it fills the landing with a white, blistering fog. The sudden blast of heat forces you to recoil, blinded by the sudden shroud of moisture that obscures both your sight and the creature's position.


## Turn 6 — I draw my service pistol and fire blindly into the steam toward the sound of the creature's movements.

You draw your Service Pistol and squeeze the trigger, firing several rounds into the thick, white shroud of steam. The muzzle flashes are muffled, swallowed instantly by the hissing roar of the ruptured conduit, and the deafening cracks of the shots echo uselessly against the metal walls. You track nothing but the frantic, wet slapping of limbs and heavy, ragged breathing that seems to come from every direction at once.

A sudden, meaty thud hits the floor inches from your feet, followed by a high-pitched, gargling shriek of agony that cuts through the steam. One of your rounds found purchase; the Pale Creature bellows, its sightless head thrashing blindly as it recoils from the impact of the bullets. The creature lunges forward, not toward you, but stumbling erratically through the blistering fog, its massive, translucent body colliding with a support pillar with enough force to rattle the entire shaft. It slumps into the white haze, its frantic movements slowing into heavy, shuddering gasps that compete with the relentless hiss of the escaping steam.


## Turn 7 — I holster my pistol and quickly scan the area for the pump control panel amidst the steam.

You holster your Service Pistol and squint through the blistering white shroud, searching for the familiar interface of the pump control panel. The scalding fog clings to your skin, making every breath a struggle against the heat, but your eyes catch the dull, metallic glint of the console's housing just beyond the spray of the ruptured conduit. As you lean forward to find the controls, the heavy, wet rasp of the Pale Creature’s breathing drifts from a narrow side-alcove nearby, muffled by the roar of the steam but unmistakably retreating into the shadows of a recessed maintenance nook.


## Turn 8 — I draw my service pistol and edge toward the console, keeping my eyes fixed on the darkness of the maintenance nook.

You draw your Service Pistol, the metal warm against your palm, and edge toward the console while keeping your eyes fixed on the darkness of the maintenance nook. You move with careful, measured steps, using the heavy steel of the pump housing as a partial shield between yourself and the wet, ragged breathing emanating from the alcove. The steam continues to swirl around your legs, thick and suffocating, but you maintain your focus on the shadow where the Pale Creature vanished.

As you reach the edge of the console, your boot catches on a loose floor grating, sending a sharp metallic clatter echoing through the shaft. The heavy breathing in the alcove stops instantly, replaced by a sudden, predatory stillness that feels heavier than the steam itself. You press your back against the console, thumbing the hammer of your Service Pistol, but as you prepare to scan the nook, a frantic shouting breaks the tension from the level above.

> "Cortez! Get away from those controls!"

The heavy thud of boots hitting the metal stairs of the landing announces the arrival of Melvin Reeves and two militia guards—the tall man with the scarred brow and his short, nervous-handed companion—as they descend into the steaming haze of the lower shafts.


## Turn 9 — I lower my pistol slightly but keep it ready, turning toward the stairs to face Melvin and the guards.

You lower the muzzle of your Service Pistol toward the floor, though your finger remains tight against the trigger guard as you pivot to face the stairs. The tall man with the scarred brow and his short, nervous-handed companion emerge from the white haze of the upper landing, their boots clanging rhythmically against the metal grating. Melvin Reeves follows them, his silhouette cutting through the swirling steam like a blunt instrument, his expression hardened by the frantic shouting that preceded his descent.

The two militia guards fan out, flanking Melvin to create a loose semi-circle that cuts off your path back up the stairs. The scarred guard keeps his rifle leveled at your chest, while the nervous man grips his weapon with white-knuckled intensity, his eyes darting between you and the dark, silent alcove where the Pale Creature hides.

Melvin Reeves stops a few paces from the console, his heavy coat damp from the humidity. He ignores the wounded creature's presence in the shadows, focusing entirely on your position by the pump controls.

"Step away from that panel, Jeffery," Melvin Reeves commands, his voice cutting through the hiss of the ruptured conduit. "You've caused enough damage to this sector for one shift."


## Turn 10 — I keep my eyes on the guards and slowly raise my hands, but I don't move away from the console.

You raise your hands slowly, palms open to show Melvin Reeves that you aren't reaching for the Service Pistol, but your feet remain planted firmly in front of the pump control panel. The scarred guard tightens his grip on his rifle, his eyes tracking every inch of your movement through the swirling white fog.

Melvin Reeves narrows his eyes, stepping closer into the heat of the steam. "I won't ask again, Jeffery. Move to the stairs or we treat this as an act of sabotage."

A wet, heavy scraping sound echoes from the alcove behind you—a low, guttural rasp that makes the nervous guard stumble backward, his rifle barrel dipping for a split second. The Pale Creature shifts in the darkness, its frantic hunger momentarily superseded by the scent of blood and the vibration of voices.

The sudden noise breaks Melvin's focus, his head snapping toward the alcove just as the creature lunges from the mist. It ignores you entirely, its sightless, milky eyes fixed on the heat of the guards. The pale entity slams into the short guard, its thick limbs wrapping around his torso and dragging him shrieking into the white haze of the landing.

"Contact! Behind us!" the scarred guard bellows, pivoting his rifle toward the darkness, but the steam is too thick to track the target.

Melvin Reeves lunges forward, not to help the fallen man, but to grab your shoulder and wrench you away from the console. "Run! Get to the upper shafts!"
