
## Turn 0 — Seed

The smell of burnt diesel and wet ash clings to the back of your throat. You can hear the rhythmic, hollow echo of boots on broken stone as your squad moves through the rubble-choked streets of South Janice. A few yards ahead, a field kitchen sits abandoned near a collapsed stone wall, its stove still radiating a faint, dying heat that cuts through the morning chill. The village is a skeleton of its former self; smoke haze from a recent fire drifts lazily between the ruins of cottages, obscuring the view of the road ahead.

You find the convoy stalled at the narrowest point of the village square. A heavy transport truck has jackknifed across the path, its chassis resting precariously against a half-collapsed beam of a nearby tavern. The driver is slumped against a wheel, his face pale as he presses a blood-soaked rag to a jagged wound on his thigh. Nearby, a church bell lies partially buried in the rubble of the parish, looking like a discarded metal skull.

'Sergeant Ryan! Get those crates moved!' Corporal Mark Lowery shouts over the crackle of a small fire nearby. He is pacing aggressively around the wreckage, his face flushed with a frantic sort of anger. He kicks at a dented fuel can, his eyes darting toward the dark treeline bordering the village. 'If we don't get this medical shipment and the fuel out of here before the scouts find us, it's our necks on the line. I didn't spend three hours hauling this through the mud just to watch it burn!'

Lowery's voice is sharp, cutting through the heavy silence that follows his outburst. You remember the last time he tried to take charge of a supply run—the argument that ended with him nearly getting your squad court-martialed for 'misplaced' rations. He looks at you now, not as a fellow veteran, but as an obstacle to his orders. Behind him, the wounded driver groans, a low and pained sound that seems to draw more attention to your position than any gunshot would. The silence of the village feels heavy, as if the ruins themselves are waiting for something to break.
