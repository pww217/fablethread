As Anthony Miller lunges toward the gutter, a heavy lantern strikes him square in the temple, the glass shattering and drenching his face in hot oil and stinging brine. Blinded by the sudden impact and the searing heat, he is shoved backward by the sheer mass of blue coats rushing into the narrow passage.

The chaos of the alley recedes as Anthony Miller is swept along by the tide of law enforcement, stumbling through a side exit and out onto the wider, more open Fourth District Streets. The transition from the cramped service alley to the main thoroughfare offers no respite; the rain continues to fall in heavy sheets, blurring the silhouettes of distant buildings. Ahead, the street is illuminated by the steady, amber glow of several storefronts, providing a stark contrast to the flickering madness he just escaped.
