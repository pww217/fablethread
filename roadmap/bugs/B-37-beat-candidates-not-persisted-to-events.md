## Detail

During E-11 beat system deep dive on zombie-survival:cautious (25t), found that beat candidate content generated by the async world step is never persisted to events.jsonl. Only the selection index (`selected_beat`) and next-turn pending beat (`post_turn_pending_beat`) are visible.

### Evidence

**Zombie-survival 25t run (15 turns examined):**
- `ruling.selected_beat` is always `0` — across all 15 turns, the ruling always selects beat index 0
- `post_turn_pending_beat` is null on 40% of turns (1, 7, 8, 11)
- When present, pending beats are predominantly `[npcs: Danny Gallegos] [highlight: leverage/motivation/fear]` — narrow NPC focus with repetitive effect patterns

### Impact

- **Debugging impossible**: Cannot trace which beats were generated, why they were filtered, or whether the LLM produced valid candidates
- **Beat diversity unknown**: Cannot assess whether the world step LLM is producing diverse beat candidates or just one valid candidate per turn
- **Beat selection opaque**: `selected_beat = 0` could mean "only one candidate" or "always picks first" — impossible to distinguish without beat candidate logs

### Suspected cause

In `ccya/engine/world.py`, beat candidates are generated and validated but the result is only passed to the ruling step via `state.meta.beat_candidates`. The ruling step uses `selected_beat` index to pick one, then clears `beat_candidates`. Neither the candidates list nor the selection rationale is persisted to events.

### Files

- `ccya/engine/world.py:31-202` — beat candidate generation and validation
- `ccya/engine/ruling.py:115-217` — beat selection from candidates
- `ccya/engine/turn.py:641-642` — event logging (only logs `post_turn_pending_beat`, not `beat_candidates`)
