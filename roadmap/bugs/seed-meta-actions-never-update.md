## Problem

`state_snapshot.__seed_meta__.actions` is set once during game initialization and never updated across all subsequent turns. The same 4 hardcoded seed actions from the pack persist through the entire game, stale and irrelevant after the first scene.

**Evidence (the-outer-rim save, 14 turns):**
Every turn's `state_snapshot.__seed_meta__.actions` contains the same 4 initial actions:
1. "Draw your service pistol and aim at the lead enforcer."
2. "Grab Daniel and attempt to retreat through the ventilation shaft."
3. "Use the plasma cutter to cause an electrical explosion near the doorway."
4. "Try to negotiate with the enforcers using your former Coalition credentials."

Meanwhile, the event-level `actions` field (generated by the storyteller) changes every turn with context-relevant predictions. The storyteller's output also contains a fresh `actions` list in `extraction.storytell.output.actions`. But neither the event-level `actions` nor the storyteller's output actions ever propagate back into `state_snapshot.__seed_meta__`.

## Reproduction

```bash
ev.py turn 1 --save-dir saves/the-outer-rim--after-unification-2026-06-24 --json | jq '.state_snapshot.__seed_meta__.actions'
ev.py turn 14 --save-dir saves/the-outer-rim--after-unification-2026-06-24 --json | jq '.state_snapshot.__seed_meta__.actions'
```

Both returns the same 4 initial actions. The event-level `actions` field differs between turns 1 and 14.

## Root Cause Estimate

The initial seed is set by the pack's `seed.yaml` (or equivalent initialization) and written into the state's `__seed_meta__` dict on game creation at `ccya/engine/seed.py` (or `init_state.py`). However, after initialization, nothing in the turn pipeline updates `state.__seed_meta__`. The storyteller generates new action predictions each turn (`extraction.storytell.output.actions`), and the event-level `actions` field stores the player's chosen/available actions for the next turn, but there is no mechanism to write these back into `state.__seed_meta__.actions`.

The `actions` field in `events.jsonl` and the storyteller's output both prove the system CAN generate per-turn predictions — they just don't persist.

## Impact

- The seed actions are never useful for convergence, beat selection, or narrative pacing past turn 1
- Any downstream system that reads `__seed_meta__.actions` (convergence, threat assessment, NPC reactions) gets stale/impossible data
- The game loses the ability to adapt its "possible futures" display as the narrative evolves
- Directly contradicts the design intent of dynamic seeding

## Suggested Fix

Add a step at the end of the turn pipeline (in `ccya/engine/step`, likely after storyteller extraction is applied) that writes the storyteller's `actions` (or the event-level `actions`) into `state.__seed_meta__.actions`. This ensures the seed meta reflects the current narrative context, not the initial scene.
