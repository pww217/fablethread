## Status

Needs scoping. Feature. Low priority (stretch goal).

## Detail

Simple karma score that increments/decrements based on player actions. Within a single game — not cross-scenario. When karma is high/low, future NPCs met may recognize the PC's reputation.

## Scope

* Add karma field to player state
* Increment/decrement based on action outcomes (evil/good deeds)
* Pass karma signal to storytell/narrate for NPC reactions
* Very early concept, no substance yet — needs serious scoping
