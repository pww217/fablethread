## Description

Bundle of prompt coherence issues rooted in `narrate_system.j2`, `narrate_user.j2`, `record_system.j2`, `world.j2`. Five findings validated across 5 eval runs (2026-07-17_0.32.1_2e3d0188). Core issue: prompts give LLM conflicting or insufficient guidance, causing beats to be ignored, record actions disconnected from narration, and generic directives.

## Findings

### I-43.1 — Beat-to-narration disconnect (CRITICAL)

Beats are generated by World step and passed as `pending_gm_beat`, but LLM ignores them 84-92% of the time. `narrate_system.j2:85` says "creative guidance — use as texture, not direction." This is explicit permission to ignore beats.

Alignment rates (keyword-based): noir-1930s 25t: 62.5%, golden-piracy 25t: 87.5%, space-western 25t: 62.5%, allied-ww2 25t: 50.0%. The original report's 8-16% claim may measure semantic alignment rather than keyword presence.

Beats persist 2-13 turns without being consumed. noir-1930s: `gilded_lily_confrontation` persists T15-T25 (11 turns). GM beats have `npcs` arrays — most contain 0-2 NPC names, not empty as previously claimed.

### I-43.2 — Record actions disconnected from narration (HIGH)

Record actions are forward-looking suggestions for what the player *might* do next, not descriptions of what the narration just depicted. noir-1930s T5: Record suggests "Offer Arthur Sterling a bribe", "Threaten with newspaper", "Inquire about shipment protection", "Search desk for documents" — but narration only matches "Threaten with newspaper".

Actions are post-hoc suggestions, not validation of narration advancement. The record prompt intentionally separates forward-looking actions from retrospective outcome_summary (record_system.j2:113-115).

### I-43.3 — Beat recycling / narrative loops (MEDIUM)

The 4-entry sliding window diversity rule in `world.j2:54-57` prevents same-type/NPC/thread in the last 4 entries, but doesn't prevent the same thread from persisting across the entire game. Same 2-3 threads dominate beat candidates across entire runs (65-84% of candidates).

Examples:
- noir: `political_exposure` in 84% of candidates (21/25 turns)
- space-western: `authority_encroachment` + `resource_scarcity` ~70% combined
- golden-piracy: `guild_enforcement` + `rival_vessel_pursuit` ~65% combined
- zombie-survival: `blockade_breach_mission` + `supply_shortage` ~70% combined

The diversity rule says "avoided if possible" not "must not appear," giving LLM discretion to repeat. Combined with "must emit at least 1" override (line 48, 52), diversity rule is effectively advisory.

### I-43.4 — arc_origin missing from per-turn prompts (MEDIUM)

`arc_origin` (2-3 sentences of backstory) is generated at seed time but never included in per-turn prompts. This causes persona drift after turn 1 — LLM loses emotional anchoring and relies solely on mechanical signals.

Verified by absence in `narrate_user.j2`, `record_system.j2`.

### I-43.5 — Directive content generic (LOW)

Directives use only two generic labels: "Scene Pressure" and "Scene Imperative". No turn has a directive with specific content like "Scene Pressure — guards are closing in."

Directive presence is 20-56% across runs (not 12% as previously claimed):
- noir-1930s 25t: 24%, space-western 25t: 24%, golden-piracy 25t: 48%, zombie-survival 25t: 56%, allied-ww2 25t: 36%

## Fix Strategy

1. Change "creative guidance" to "MANDATORY scene element" in `narrate_system.j2` — require LLM to weave beats into narration
2. Restructure `record_system.j2` — first describe what narration depicted, then suggest next actions (reverse current order)
3. Increase diversity window from 4 to 8-10 in `world.j2` — consider thread cooldowns (6-8 turns)
4. Reintroduce `arc_origin` into narrate/record prompts (even condensed, 1 sentence)
5. Make directive generation produce specific, context-aware directives
